--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Experimental Sources:
1. NIST Standard Reference Database: NIST Chemistry WebBook (SRD 69), Phase
   Transitions & High-Temperature Thermodynamic Properties of Silicon Nitride.
2. Khan, S., et al. (2014). "High tensile strength silicon nitride membranes
   for nanomechanical resonators", Applied Physics Letters, Vol. 105, Iss. 8, 083109.
   DOI: 10.1063/1.4894269
3. Atwater, H. A., Lubin, P., et al. (2020). "Materials Challenges for the
   Starshot Lightsail", Nano Letters, Vol. 20, No. 10, pp. 6808-6815.
   DOI: 10.1021/acs.nanolett.0c02517

Verified Physical & Mechanical Properties:
- Density (rho): 3,100 kg/m^3 (3.10 g/cm^3)
- Young's Modulus (E): 250.0 to 290.0 GPa (Project Setu baseline: 250.0 GPa)
- Poisson's Ratio (nu): 0.23
- Ultimate Tensile Yield Strength (sigma_y): 1.80 to 2.40 GPa (Project Setu baseline: 2.00 GPa / 2,000 MPa)
- Thermal Conductivity (k): 30.0 W/(m*K) (In-plane thin film)
- Coefficient of Thermal Expansion (alpha): 3.0 x 10^-6 K^-1
- Sublimation / Breakdown Temperature: 2,170.0 K (1,896.85 deg C)
- Refractive Index at lambda = 1064 nm (n): 2.00
- Extinction Coefficient (kappa): < 10^-6 (Near-zero absorption at 1064 nm)
